Artificial intelligence is becoming an important part of modern software and business operations. 
Organizations use AI systems to analyze information, automate repetitive tasks, improve customer service, 
and support decision making. The rapid growth of machine learning has made it possible for computers to 
identify patterns in large datasets and produce useful predictions.

Natural language processing is one important area of artificial intelligence. NLP allows computers to work 
with human language in documents, messages, websites, and other sources. Common NLP tasks include tokenization, 
classification, sentiment analysis, information extraction, translation, and summarization. These techniques 
help organizations convert large amounts of unstructured text into information that is easier to understand.

Document summarization can be divided into extractive and abstractive approaches. Extractive summarization 
selects important sentences from the original document. Abstractive summarization attempts to generate new 
sentences that express the meaning of the source material. Extractive methods are often easier to implement 
because they do not require a large language model or a large training dataset.

A classical extractive summarizer can calculate the frequency of meaningful words in a document. Sentences 
containing important words can then receive higher scores. The system selects the highest scoring sentences 
and places them back into their original order. This approach can create useful summaries while remaining 
lightweight enough to run on an ordinary computer.

Flask is a popular Python framework for building web applications. It can receive uploaded documents, call 
document processing functions, run NLP algorithms, and return results to a browser. HTML, CSS, and JavaScript 
can then provide a user-friendly interface for uploading files and displaying summaries.

Privacy is another important consideration for document analysis. A local summarization application can 
process uploaded documents on the user's own computer rather than sending their content to a third-party 
cloud service. This can be useful for private notes, internal reports, academic documents, and other material 
that should not be shared externally.

AI document summarization does not replace careful reading in every situation. Important legal, medical, 
financial, and technical decisions may require the complete original document. A summary should therefore 
be treated as a tool for quickly understanding the main ideas and deciding which parts of a document deserve 
closer attention.
